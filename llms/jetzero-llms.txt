# JetZero

> JetZero is a Long Beach, California aerospace company founded in 2021 by Tom O'Leary and Mark Page to bring the blended wing body (all-wing) airframe to commercial aviation. Its flagship program is the Z4, a 250-seat, international-range jetliner the company says will burn up to 50% less fuel than comparable tube-and-wing airliners, alongside the Jet1 full-scale demonstrator being built with Scaled Composites in Mojave, California ahead of a first flight targeted for 2027.

JetZero is an aircraft manufacturer, not a software or data provider. As of 2026-08-01 it publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDKs, no OpenAPI/GraphQL/AsyncAPI contract, no MCP server, and no A2A agent card**. Every discovery path was probed and missed (see `well-known/jetzero-well-known.yml`). The company's public technical surface is a supplier registration and supplier standards program.
